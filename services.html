The Services We Offer.

Maintenance

Regular maintenance keeps your car reliable. Most cars should be checked around every 10,000 miles. Performance-driven or modified cars may need service every 3,000 to 5,000 miles.

Diagnostics

If your car has a no-start, boost leak, overheating, a misfire, unusual noises, or electrical problems, bring it in immediately. We offer diagnostics and maintenance in-house.

Brakes + Suspension

We handle brake jobs, pads, rotors, fluid, coilovers, bushings, control arms, alignment preparation, and other brake or suspension service your car may need.

Turbo Systems

We work on custom turbo systems, intercooler piping, intake routing, oil and coolant lines, clamps, and heat management to help your setup make reliable power.

Performance Installs

We install performance parts, supporting parts, intake and exhaust components, suspension upgrades, cooling upgrades, and other parts for your build.
